About Us

Golden Crust began as a small family kitchen with one oven and a big dream. Today we still bake everything by hand, every day, using simple ingredients and recipes passed down through generations.

Our dough rests overnight and our pastries are folded by hand, whether it’s your morning loaf or a wedding cake.
